Common questions

What are the OPD timings?

Morning OPD runs from 10:00 am to 2:00 pm and evening OPD from 5:00 pm to 7:00 pm. Emergency and NICU are open 24 hours, every day.

Is treatment free under the Ayushman scheme?

Luv Kush Hospital treats patients under the Mukhyamantri Ayushman Arogya Yojana. Eligible families get cashless treatment for covered procedures. Bring your Jan Aadhaar card and our help desk will confirm what is covered.

Can I come at night in an emergency?

Yes. Emergency is open 24 hours. Call 81077 81966 on your way so the team can get ready.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the booking form on this page to send your details on WhatsApp, or call 81077 81966. You can also walk in during OPD hours.

Is sonography done at the hospital?

Yes. Pregnancy scans, TVS and colour Doppler sonography are done in-house.
